Meet the Doctor

Dr. Dechowat Promda

Dechowat Promda, M.D. · The Filler Architect

The founding physician of Vinfinity Clinic. Since graduating from medical school, he has worked in two fields: aesthetic medicine and digital health data systems. Both have shaped the same approach to patient care: use data to be precise, then use a trained eye to judge the result.

Philosophy

Data-driven precision.
Art-driven result.

Data shows where to correct. Art shows where to stop. The two have to work together for a face to look better and still look like you.

01 · DATA-DRIVEN PRECISION

Precision through data

  • A 3D facial scan and a layer-by-layer reading of your facial structure before every plan
  • Products chosen for their gel properties and research evidence, not their brand name
  • The product, amount and placement recorded every time, so the next treatment is more precise
  • A background in AI in Healthcare (NUS) and in building national health data systems
02 · ART-DRIVEN RESULT

Beauty through art

  • Looking at each face's proportions, light and shadow, rather than one formula for everyone
  • Knowing where to stop, and using only what is needed
  • Results that look refreshed without looking "done"
  • Best Result on Difficult Case award, Galderma Thailand, 2021
Two worlds, one method

Two roles, one way of thinking

AESTHETIC MEDICINE

Aesthetic medicine

Founded Vinfinity Clinic in Udon Thani and Vientiane. Created The Filler Architect concept and The Architect Rebuild program, which plans treatment across 5 layers. Selected as a KOL by global brands Galderma · Vivacy · MNB · IMCAS.

DIGITAL HEALTH

Digital health data

Founder and CEO of HealthTAG, a developer of health data infrastructure. He previously founded Medicense Intelligence, ran an HL7 FHIR project with SIL-TH, and was a researcher in Biomedical & Precision Medicine at Mahidol University.
