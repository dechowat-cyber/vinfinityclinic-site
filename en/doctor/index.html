Philosophy

Data-driven precision.
Art-driven result.

“Data tells you where to treat. Art tells you where to stop. You need both for a face to look better and still look like you.”Dr. Dechowat Promda

People often ask how a doctor who builds health data systems ended up injecting faces. For him it has always been one story: it starts with data, becomes precision, is raised to the latest global standards, and ends as art on each individual face.

01 · DATA

It starts with data

After medical school, he chose to work on health data alongside patient care. He founded Medicense Intelligence, ran an HL7 FHIR standards project with SIL-TH, and then founded HealthTAG, which builds health data infrastructure.

The lesson from that work: good data has to be verifiable, and it has to lead to real decisions.

  • Founder & CEO, HealthTAG
  • HL7 FHIR × SIL-TH
  • TEDx “Don’t Trust, Just Verify”
02 · PRECISION

Data becomes precision

He was a researcher in Biomedical & Precision Medicine at Mahidol University and studied AI in Healthcare at the National University of Singapore. This work has won awards in Asia and Europe.

In the clinic, precision means a 3D facial scan, reading your structure layer by layer before planning, and recording the product, amount and placement every time, so each visit is more precise than the last.

  • ODESS Winner 2024 · France
  • MEDICA Top 12 · 2023
  • AI in Healthcare · NUS
03 · STATE OF THE ART

Keeping pace with global standards

In aesthetic medicine, he was selected as a KOL by global brands Galderma · Vivacy · MNB and IMCAS, attended Galderma's GAIN JPAC 2024 in Seoul, and trained with the manufacturer in France in 2025.

What he brings back: choosing products for their gel properties and research evidence, not the brand name, and writing it up as research-based articles patients can read.

  • KOL Galderma · Vivacy · MNB · IMCAS
  • GAIN JPAC 2024 · Seoul
  • Vivacy · Paris 2025
04 · ART

And it ends as art

Data can tell you where to treat, but knowing where to stop takes skill and a trained eye, and both are meant to be passed on. He took part in a Live Demonstration at IMCAS Asia 2023, joined the team at the Kysse Road Show by Galderma Thailand physician training in 2022, has trained physicians in Northeast Thailand for Galderma, and received the Best Result on Difficult Case award from Galderma Thailand in 2021.

The goal: you look refreshed without looking done, and still like yourself.

  • Live Demonstration · IMCAS Asia 2023
  • Kysse Road Show · Galderma 2022
  • Galderma AART trainer
  • Best Result on Difficult Case 2021

All of this comes together in the treatment room at Vinfinity Clinic in Udon Thani and Vientiane, through The Filler Architect approach and The Architect Rebuild program, which plans treatment across 5 layers, with the doctor assessing and injecting every patient personally.

FAQ

About the doctor

Who is Dr. Bas, Dr. Dechowat Promda?

The founding physician of Vinfinity Clinic in Udon Thani and Vientiane, creator of The Filler Architect approach that plans facial treatment across 5 layers, and founder of HealthTAG, a digital health data company.

Where did the doctor study?

Doctor of Medicine, Khon Kaen University (2014), and a Certification in AI in Healthcare from the National University of Singapore (2023).

How can I verify the doctor's license?

Medical license no. 48943. You can check the name on the Medical Council of Thailand website.

Does the doctor inject every patient personally?

Yes. A physician assesses and treats every patient. Injections are never delegated to non-physicians.

What is The Filler Architect?

The doctor's approach of reading the face like architecture: assessing each layer from bone to skin, planning what each layer needs, using only what is necessary, and following up until the plan is complete.

Where does the doctor teach or speak?

He has trained physicians in Northeast Thailand for Galderma, served as faculty at IMCAS Asia, and lectures on Digital Health master's programs at KMITL, Chulalongkorn University and Khon Kaen University.
