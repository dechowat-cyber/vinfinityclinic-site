Your doctor

Dechowat Promda, M.D. (Thai Medical Council licence 48943) graduated in medicine from Khon Kaen University. He developed the "Filler Architect" approach, which treats the face as five anatomical layers, from skin to bone. He has been selected as a key opinion leader by global aesthetic brands and has spoken at international aesthetic meetings.

Safety you can check
