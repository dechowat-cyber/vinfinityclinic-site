Our Story

Why we call ourselves Architects

“Good skin doesn’t come from the most expensive product. It comes from choosing the right thing for each layer.”DR. DECHOWAT PROMDA

Many people have had dermal filler and felt their face looked “fuller”, but not actually better. Or they’ve had a skin booster and enjoyed that dewy glow for only a few weeks. The problem usually isn’t the product. It’s treating the wrong layer.

Your face is like a building. It has a structure, supports that hold everything firmly in place, soft tissue, and an outer surface. As the deeper layers change with age, the layers above start to sag with them. Filling only at the surface can’t reach the root cause.

So at Vinfinity, every case starts with reading your facial structure. Then we design what each layer needs, use only what is necessary, and follow up with you until the plan is complete.

The Architecture of a Face

The 5 layers of the face a doctor must read before injecting

Tap each layer to see what sits there and how it is treated. Major blood vessels run at several depths, so knowing exactly which layer the needle tip is in matters for both results and safety.

Epidermis · Dermis

Skin

The layer you can see and touch. Hydration, firmness and skin texture all live here.

Skin boosters (PN · HA) injected into the skin →
Superficial fat compartments

Superficial fat

Fat divided into compartments by thin walls, which moves with your facial expressions. Fill it with a gel that is too firm and the face can look tight or “overfilled” when you smile.

Small amounts of a highly flexible gel →
Superficial musculoaponeurotic system

SMAS · muscle

A sheet of fibrous tissue and muscle that holds the face in shape. When it loosens, the cheeks and jawline sag with it.

Lifting with focused energy (New Doublo 2.0) →
Deep fat compartments · Retaining ligaments

Deep fat · ligaments

Deep fat forms the supporting base, and the retaining ligaments are the anchors that tie the skin to the bone. When this layer deflates, the layers above sag along with it.

High-G′ gel or collagen stimulation →
Bone · Periosteum

Bone · periosteum

The framework that sets your facial proportions: cheekbones, temples, chin and jawline. Bone itself also changes with age.

Structural dermal filler placed on the periosteum →

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who performs the treatments?

A doctor assesses and treats every patient personally. Dr. Dechowat Promda oversees treatment planning at the clinic.

What is the difference between dermal filler and a skin booster?

Dermal filler restores volume or refines structure in specific areas, while a skin booster improves skin quality, such as hydration and firmness. The two are often combined in a layered treatment plan.

How many sessions will I need to see results?

It depends on the treatment and on your skin. After your assessment, the doctor will tell you how many sessions you need and how far apart they should be.

How long do results last?

It depends on the product, the treatment area and your own metabolism. The doctor will suggest a follow-up schedule that suits you.
