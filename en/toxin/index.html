Anti-wrinkle injections in Udon Thani
Softer lines and a slimmer face, with natural movement

Botulinum toxin injections (often called anti-wrinkle injections) soften lines on the forehead, between the brows and at the corners of the eyes, and can slim the jaw for a more tapered face. At Vinfinity Clinic in Udon Thani, the doctor always checks how your muscles move before deciding on the number of units.

Summary

  • Botulinum toxin reduces the activity of specific muscles, so expression lines look softer and enlarged jaw muscles gradually become smaller.
  • Results appear within 3–14 days and last about 3–6 months. Injection takes 5–10 minutes, with no downtime.
  • There are 2 product tiers, Standard (Korea) and Premium (Europe/USA), with prices starting from THB 1,500 per area.

Why your face shouldn't look frozen

A frozen face or drooping brows usually comes from using more units than a person's muscles need, or injecting in the wrong spot. At Vinfinity, the doctor asks you to move your face before injecting, checks the strength of each muscle, and then sets the units and injection points for you individually. The goal is softer lines while you can still smile and express yourself naturally.

Botulinum toxin treats lines caused by movement. Deep lines that are visible even when your face is at rest usually need dermal filler or a skin booster as well, and the doctor can plan everything together in one visit.

Who should not have it

  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Myasthenia gravis or other neuromuscular disorders
  • Infection at the injection site, or allergy to any ingredient in the product
  • Certain medications that affect the muscles; tell the doctor about all the medicines you take

After anti-wrinkle injections

  • For the first 4 hours, avoid lying flat, bending your head down or strenuous exercise.
  • On the first day, avoid massaging, pressing or scrubbing the treated area.
  • Results start to show within 3–14 days. If things still look uneven after 2 weeks, let the doctor know so they can assess it.

Anti-wrinkle injection FAQ

How much are anti-wrinkle injections in Udon Thani?

At Vinfinity Clinic prices start at THB 1,500 per area, for example THB 1,900 between the brows and THB 4,900 for jaw slimming (Standard). You can also choose a Premium product from Europe/America.

How soon will I see results from botulinum toxin?

Most people start to see results within 3–14 days. The jaw slims gradually and becomes more noticeable over several weeks.

How long do anti-wrinkle injections last?

About 3–6 months, depending on the area, the size of the muscle and your metabolism.

Will my face look frozen?

When the units and injection points are tailored to your muscles, your face can still move naturally. The doctor asks you to make facial expressions before every treatment.

What is the difference between Standard and Premium?

Standard is a quality product from Korea, and Premium is a product from Europe/America. The doctor can advise you based on the area and what you want.

Results vary from person to person, depending on muscle characteristics and the doctor's clinical judgement. Botulinum toxin injections carry risks. Treatment should be performed by a doctor in a licensed medical facility.

Want to know how many units you need?

Record a short video of yourself raising your eyebrows, frowning and smiling, and send it on LINE. The doctor will give you an initial assessment.
