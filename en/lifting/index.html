Summary

  • HIFU delivers ultrasound energy to the layers beneath the skin to stimulate the tissue to tighten, without surgery.
  • New Doublo 2.0 offers cartridges for several depths (FL / SD / RM), treating the fascia layer, the fat layer and the skin.
  • It suits mild to moderate laxity. The doctor first assesses whether you need lifting, structural support with filler, or a combination of both.

How HIFU lifting works

The device focuses ultrasound waves at a set depth, creating tiny points of heat beneath the skin without damaging the surface. The body repairs these areas and keeps producing new collagen for several weeks, so the result becomes gradually more visible after treatment.

Treating several depths in one session (multilayer) means both the supporting layers and the skin itself are addressed, in line with the clinic's 5-layer facial anatomy approach.

Who it suits

  • People whose facial contour is becoming less defined, whose cheeks are starting to sag, or who have mild jowling under the jaw.
  • People who want a firmer-looking face without surgery or long downtime.
  • Not suitable for: pregnancy, wounds or infection in the treatment area, metal or electronic implants in the face, or laxity so advanced that surgery is more appropriate.

Choosing Double or Triple Firm

The doctor assesses your skin thickness, fat volume and degree of laxity, then chooses the cartridges and number of passes for each area. The whole face is not treated the same way. Areas with little fat, or hollow cheeks, are avoided so the face doesn't look gaunt.

Lifting combined with filler and skin boosters

If the face is sagging because the underlying structure has lost volume, lifting alone may not be enough. Many treatment plans therefore combine approaches: restoring structure with dermal filler, lifting with HIFU, then improving skin quality with a skin booster.

Skin lifting FAQ

How much does skin lifting cost in Udon Thani?

New Doublo 2.0 at Vinfinity Clinic starts at THB 22,222. The final price depends on the depth levels and areas treated, and the doctor confirms the full cost before starting.

Does HIFU hurt?

You may feel pulses of heat or tightness, especially near bone. How it feels depends on the energy level and on the person.

When will I see results from HIFU?

Some people feel tighter straight after treatment, but the effect of new collagen usually becomes clearer gradually, over several weeks to a few months.

Will lifting make my face look hollow?

The doctor chooses the depth levels and avoids areas with little fat or hollow cheeks. If needed, filler may be planned alongside to support the structure.

Is there any downtime?

Most people return to their normal routine straight away. There may be mild, short-lived redness or swelling.

Results vary from person to person, depending on skin condition and the doctor's clinical judgement. Every procedure can have side effects. Please consult a doctor before deciding.
