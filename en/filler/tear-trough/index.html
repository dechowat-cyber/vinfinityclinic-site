Summary

  • Tear trough filler uses a soft hyaluronic acid (HA) gel to fill the hollow left when under-eye tissue loses volume, smoothing the junction between the lower eyelid and the cheek.
  • Not everyone with dark circles or eye bags is a good candidate for filler. The doctor first needs to tell whether the cause is a hollow, a fat pad, pigmentation or visible blood vessels.
  • At Vinfinity Clinic in Udon Thani, a doctor assesses and injects every case personally, uses small amounts, and reviews the result before deciding whether to add more.

What causes a tear trough?

With age, the fat and supporting tissue under the eyes thins out, and the bone around the eye socket changes shape. This creates a shadowed groove between the lower eyelid and the cheek, so you look tired even after a good night's sleep. Some people have this groove from a young age simply because of their natural facial structure.

Who it suits, and who it doesn't

  • Good candidates: people with a clear, shadowed tear trough, under-eye skin that is still thick enough, and a wish for natural-looking results.
  • Use caution or not suitable: frequent under-eye puffiness or fluid retention, very large fat bags, very thin and lax under-eye skin, infection at the injection site, pregnancy or breastfeeding.

If filler is not right for you, the doctor will tell you honestly and suggest other options, such as restoring the upper cheek first, using a skin booster to improve skin quality, or referring you to consider surgery.

How it works at Vinfinity Clinic, Udon Thani

  1. Assessment The doctor looks at your under-eye area in light from several directions, from the front and the side, to identify which layer the shadow comes from.
  2. Planning You are told the amount, the injection points and the full cost before anything starts. For some people the upper cheek needs support first before the under-eye area will look right.
  3. Product check in front of you Only Thai FDA-registered products are used. The box is opened in front of you so you can check it before injection.
  4. Injection Small amounts are injected slowly, in the layer that suits your skin. Filler-dissolving enzyme is kept on hand at the clinic.
  5. Follow-up At around 2 weeks, once all swelling has settled, the doctor reviews whether any top-up is needed.

After tear trough filler

  • You may have mild swelling or bruising for 2–7 days. Gentle cold compresses are fine.
  • Sleep with your head raised on the first night, and avoid massaging or pressing the treated area.
  • If pain keeps getting worse, the skin turns pale or changes colour, or you notice any vision problems, contact the clinic immediately.

Tear trough filler FAQ

How much does tear trough filler cost in Udon Thani?

Filler at Vinfinity Clinic starts at THB 9,990 per cc. The amount needed under the eyes depends on how deep your tear trough is. The doctor confirms the full cost after your assessment, before treatment.

Does tear trough filler hurt?

A numbing cream is applied first, and most products contain a local anesthetic. Most people feel only slight tightness or pressure during the injection.

How long does tear trough filler last?

It depends on the product and your metabolism. The under-eye area moves relatively little, so results often last longer than in some other areas. The doctor will see you for regular follow-ups.

How long does swelling last after tear trough filler?

Most people have mild swelling or bruising for 2–7 days. Some people retain fluid under the eyes more easily than others, and the doctor assesses this before injecting.

I have large under-eye bags. Can filler help?

In some cases filler can smooth the junction between the bag and the cheek, but if the fat bags are very large, surgery may be more suitable. The doctor will tell you honestly after the assessment.

Can tear trough filler be corrected if I'm not happy with it?

Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with an enzyme by a doctor. Please come back so the doctor can assess you first.

Results vary from person to person, depending on skin condition and the doctor's clinical judgement. Filler injections in any area carry risks. Treatment should be performed by a doctor in a licensed medical facility.
