Summary

  • Chin filler uses a hyaluronic acid (HA) gel with good structural support, injected deep near the bone to add length or projection to the chin.
  • The doctor looks at the three thirds of your face and your side profile. The goal is a balanced face, not the same pointed chin for everyone.
  • Prices start from THB 9,990 per cc. No surgery is needed, the change is visible straight after treatment, and it can be adjusted with enzyme if needed.

A chin that is too long or too pointed for your face looks obviously done. So the doctor designs it to suit your nose, lips and jaw, and often assesses it together with lip filler.

Who it suits, and who it doesn't

  • Good candidates: a short or receding chin, a short lower face, lips that look prominent in profile, and a wish to see a change straight away without surgery.
  • Use caution or not suitable: infection or inflamed acne on the chin, pregnancy or breastfeeding. If you already have silicone or another substance in your chin, tell the doctor first.

After chin filler

  • You may have mild swelling or tenderness for 1–3 days.
  • For 1 week, avoid massaging or pressing the chin, or sleeping face-down on it. Avoid spicy food and alcohol for 24 hours.
  • If pain keeps getting worse, or the skin turns pale or develops a dark patch, contact the clinic immediately.

Chin filler FAQ

How much does chin filler cost in Udon Thani?

European HA filler starts at THB 9,990 per cc. The amount depends on the length and shape that need adjusting. The doctor confirms the full cost before starting.

How long does chin filler last?

About 6–12 months, or longer for some people, because it is placed deep near the bone in an area that moves little.

Will chin filler make my chin pointy?

Not necessarily. The doctor designs the shape around your facial structure. Some faces suit a rounded chin, while others suit a slightly longer one.

How is chin filler different from a silicone chin implant?

Filler needs no surgery, shows results immediately and can be adjusted. A silicone implant is a surgical procedure with longer-lasting results, but it is harder to correct.

I've had chin augmentation before. Can I have filler?

Please tell the doctor what you have had done before. The doctor will assess whether filler can be injected safely.

Results vary from person to person, depending on skin condition and the doctor's clinical judgement. Filler injections in any area carry risks. Treatment should be performed by a doctor in a licensed medical facility.
