What is dermal filler?

Most fillers used in aesthetics are hyaluronic acid (HA) gels. HA is a substance your body already makes naturally. Filler is used to restore areas that have hollowed out or to support structure that is missing, and over time the body gradually breaks it down on its own.

Each gel has a different firmness and flexibility. Firmer gels suit structural work such as the chin and jawline, while softer gels suit thin-skinned areas such as under the eyes. Matching the gel to the right layer and area matters as much as injection technique. Read more in our article on why the same filler gives different results.

The Filler Architect approach

A deep nasolabial fold does not mean the filler has to go into the fold. Often the real cause is an upper cheek that has deflated with age. Filling the fold directly can make the face look heavier without making it look any younger.

That is why Dr. Dechowat looks at the face as a structure of several layers. He starts with the points that support the face, then refines the details, uses only as much as is needed, and reviews the result before deciding whether to add more.

  • Assess facial proportions from the front and the side
  • Choose the gel to match the layer and area
  • Plan in stages; there is no need to treat every area in one session

Safety

  • The doctor personally assesses and injects every case
  • We use products registered with the Thai FDA and open the box in front of you so you can check it before injecting
  • Filler-dissolving enzyme and a complication management protocol are kept ready in the clinic
  • Tell the doctor about any drug allergies, medical conditions and current medications during your assessment

Filler in any area carries risks, such as swelling, bruising, lumps, or vascular occlusion, which is rare but needs immediate treatment. Always choose a doctor working in a licensed medical facility.

After your filler treatment

  • Mild swelling or bruising may last 2–7 days; cold compresses help
  • Avoid massaging or pressing the treated area, and avoid saunas or strenuous exercise in the first days, as your doctor advises
  • Come back for your follow-up as scheduled by the doctor before deciding on any top-up

Filler often stays in the face longer than you can see, so every top-up should be reassessed. Read more in our article on why filler lasts longer than you think.

Does filler injection hurt?

In most cases a numbing cream is applied first, and many products also contain a local anesthetic. How it feels depends on the area and on the person.

Is there any downtime after filler?

Most people return to their normal routine straight away. Mild swelling or bruising may last 2–7 days. The doctor gives you personal aftercare advice.

How long does filler last?

Hyaluronic acid (HA) filler generally lasts about 6–18 months, depending on the product, the area and your metabolism.

Can it be corrected if I'm not happy with the result?

Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with an enzyme by a doctor. Please see a doctor for an assessment first.
