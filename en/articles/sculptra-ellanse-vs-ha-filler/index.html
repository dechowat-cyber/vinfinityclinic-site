Sculptra, Ellansé or HA filler:
how do they differ, and which to choose?

All three are injected, but they work in different ways. One adds volume immediately; the other two stimulate the skin to make its own collagen. Choosing the wrong type can give a result that doesn't match what you wanted.

By Dechowat Promda, M.D. · Updated 7 October 2026 · 7 min read

Key points Side-by-side comparison HA filler Sculptra (PLLA) Ellansé (PCL) Which to choose Safety How we use them at Vinfinity References

Key points

  • HA filler adds volume with immediate results, suits targeted reshaping, and can be dissolved with an enzyme.
  • Sculptra (PLLA) does not fill immediately; instead it gradually stimulates the skin to produce its own collagen. It needs 2–4 sessions, and results last about 2 years.
  • Ellansé (PCL) gives immediate volume from its carrier gel, which is then gradually replaced by collagen around the particles. The S version lasts at least 18 months and the M version at least 24 months.
  • Sculptra and Ellansé cannot be dissolved with an enzyme, so they are not suitable for first-time patients who are still unsure, or for areas that demand high precision.

HA filler: immediate and reversible

Hyaluronic acid filler gives results as soon as it is injected, so it suits work that requires control over shape, such as the under-eyes, lips or chin. A key advantage is that if you are unhappy with the result or a problem arises, it can be dissolved with an enzyme. Read more in our article on dissolving filler.

What many people don't realise is that the gel can still be detected on MRI long after it stops being visible.5 Every top-up should therefore be based on a fresh assessment.

Sculptra (PLLA): letting the skin build its own collagen

A 2025 review in Aesthetic Surgery Journal explains that PLLA triggers a controlled inflammatory response that leads fibroblasts to produce more type I collagen. This process takes time: collagen gradually forms between weeks 6 and 16, and can remain stable for up to about 24 months.1

  • Usually 2–4 sessions, 4–6 weeks apart, with results lasting about 2–3 years, depending on the individual and the treatment plan.1
  • A 2025 systematic review found results lasting up to 25 months.2
  • It cannot be dissolved with an enzyme; the particles are gradually broken down by the body over time.1

It suits people whose whole face is starting to look hollow or lax, and who are happy to wait for a gradual result. See details and prices on our Sculptra in Udon Thani page.

Ellansé (PCL): immediate volume and long-lasting results

Ellansé consists of 30% polycaprolactone particles in a 70% carboxymethyl cellulose gel. The gel provides immediate volume and is absorbed over 2–3 months, during which collagen forms around the particles, so the result is maintained.3

  • The S version lasts at least 18 months and the M version at least 24 months.3
  • A small study of 9 patients treated in the cheek area, measured with 3D imaging, found that volume was still increased at 2 years.4 The sample is small, however, so the findings should be interpreted with caution.
  • The authors recommend avoiding the lips, the glabella and the periorbital area, including under the eyes.3

In practice, many plans use more than one type in the same face, with each placed in the layer that suits it, following the principle of layered injection.

Safety

  • Lumps or nodules under the skin: these can occur with collagen stimulators. For PLLA, a 2025 review reports that they have become much less common with higher dilution volumes and improved technique.1 For Ellansé, post-marketing data from 2009–2017 showed about 1 adverse event per 1,769 syringes.3
  • Vascular occlusion: this is a risk with every type of injection, and products that cannot be dissolved with an enzyme are harder to correct. The doctor therefore needs a thorough knowledge of anatomy and must choose the correct layer. Read more in our article on filler safety.
  • An unsatisfactory result: HA can be corrected by dissolving it, whereas with Sculptra and Ellansé you have to wait for the body to break them down. Choosing correctly from the start is therefore important.

How we use them at Vinfinity
