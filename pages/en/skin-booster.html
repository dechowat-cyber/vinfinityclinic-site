What is a skin booster?

A skin booster delivers nourishing ingredients directly into the skin to improve skin quality. Unlike dermal filler, it is not meant to change the shape of your face. The results people usually notice are better-hydrated skin, a smoother texture and a fresher, more radiant look.

Tired-looking skin is rarely just a surface problem. When the inner layers of the skin lack water and collagen, creams applied from outside cannot reach them. Skin boosters therefore treat layer 4 in Vinfinity's 5-layer approach.

Types of skin booster

The doctor chooses the type and injection depth based on your skin. In some cases, more than one type is used within the same course.

Who it suits

  • Dry, dehydrated skin where makeup doesn't sit well
  • Tired, dull skin that lacks a healthy glow
  • Fine lines, or skin that is starting to lose elasticity
  • People who want to care for their skin alongside lifting or dermal filler

Course and aftercare

Skin boosters are usually planned as a course of several sessions, then repeated periodically to maintain the skin. The doctor sets the number of sessions and the interval after assessing your skin.

  • You may have small bumps or redness at the injection points for 1–3 days.
  • Avoid makeup on the treated area on the first day, and use sunscreen regularly.
  • Tell the doctor if you notice anything unusual after treatment.

Results vary from person to person, depending on skin condition and how you care for your skin. Every type of injection can have side effects. Please consult a doctor before deciding.

What it can be combined with

Skin boosters improve skin quality, but they do not lift or reshape the face. If you also have laxity or loss of facial structure, the doctor will often plan treatment together with:

  • New Doublo 2.0 lifting for the supporting (tightening) layer
  • Dermal filler for the structural layer
  • The Architect Rebuild, a 12-week programme that covers all 5 layers
