Our Story

Why we call ourselves Architects

“Good skin doesn’t come from the most expensive product. It comes from choosing the right thing for each layer.”DR. DECHOWAT PROMDA

Many people have had dermal filler and felt their face looked “fuller”, but not actually better. Or they’ve had a skin booster and enjoyed that dewy glow for only a few weeks. The problem usually isn’t the product. It’s treating the wrong layer.

Your face is like a building. It has a structure, supports that hold everything firmly in place, soft tissue, and an outer surface. As the deeper layers change with age, the layers above start to sag with them. Filling only at the surface can’t reach the root cause.

So at Vinfinity, every case starts with reading your facial structure. Then we design what each layer needs, use only what is necessary, and follow up with you until the plan is complete.
