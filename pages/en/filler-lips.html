Summary

  • Lip filler uses a soft hyaluronic acid (HA) gel that moves naturally with your lips. It can adjust volume, define the lip border and lift downturned corners.
  • Attractive lips are in proportion with the face, with an upper-to-lower lip ratio of about 1 to 1.6, and they need to look good from both the front and the side.
  • Prices start from THB 9,990 per cc. Most people start with a small amount, then come back to review the result before deciding whether to add more.

How we design a natural-looking lip shape

What you want to changeWhat the doctor looks at
Thin lipsThe height of the upper and lower lips, and the fullness that suits your face shape
Undefined lip borderThe lip line and Cupid's bow, without overfilling into a hard edge
Uneven lipsThe difference between each side, both at rest and when smiling
Downturned mouth cornersThe structure around the mouth and chin; some people also benefit from chin or marionette line filler

Lips that project too far beyond the face in profile are what the doctor guards against most, so the lips are always assessed together with the chin and nose.

Who it suits, and who it doesn't

  • Good candidates: thin lips, an undefined lip shape, uneven lips, or slightly downturned mouth corners.
  • Use caution or not suitable: active cold sores (herpes) or an infected wound on the lips, pregnancy or breastfeeding, or a wish for lips far larger than the face's proportions.

If you have had cold sores before, let the doctor know in advance, as you may need preventive medication before injection.

  4. Injection Numbing cream is applied first. Treatment takes about 15 minutes. Filler-dissolving enzyme is kept on hand at the clinic.
  5. Follow-up At around 2 weeks, once all swelling has settled.

After lip filler

  • Swelling peaks in the first 1–2 days, then gradually settles over 3–7 days. Your true lip shape shows clearly at around 2 weeks.
  • Cold compresses are fine. Avoid sucking hard through a straw, very hot food and alcohol for 24 hours.
  • If pain keeps getting worse, or the skin turns pale or develops a dark patch, contact the clinic immediately.
